**You see:** your own LGA, restricted records (and your own records).

## Every day

- Check the dashboard and the bell (returned incidents, reminders).
- **Report incidents:** Incidents → Report incident. Save as draft any time.
- **Critical?** Core facts only (type, title, description, severity
  Critical, time, LGA) → **Submit**. Escalated automatically. Complete it
  afterwards.
- Fix and resubmit **returned** incidents.
- **Add update** on the Timeline tab as you learn more.
- **Record threat report** for warnings. Never record an informant's identity.

## Regularly

- **Coverage report** for each period: Security → Coverage reports → New report.
- Flashpoint, blackspot and person of interest **reviews** when due.
- Keep the LGA profile and communities current.

## Persons

- Search by NIN, phone, or name **with date of birth**.
- "Record found" / "Likely match" in another state → **Request access**
  (reason of 30+ characters, scope, urgency, 7/14/30 days).
- Grants are read-only and time-limited.

## Rules

- Lowest classification that protects the information.
- Everything you view, change, print or export is recorded.
- Sign out on shared computers. Idle timeout 30 minutes.
- Lost phone or locked out → your system administrator.
