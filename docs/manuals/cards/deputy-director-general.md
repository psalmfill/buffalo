**You see:** national, or your assigned zones, up to secret.

## Every day

- **Acknowledge** escalated incidents from your states; follow them.
- Act on critical notifications: critical incidents, immediate threats,
  critical coverage, overdue verifications.
- **Access requests → Oversight:** requests and grants in your states;
  decide escalated requests.
- **Administration → Exports review:** who exported what, and when.

## You can

- Everything a State Director can, across your states (except deciding
  requests that are not escalated).
- Reopen, close and invalidate incidents.
- Analytics, including cross-jurisdiction activity.

## Rules

- National users receive no "record found" notices: you already see every
  record in reach.
- Everything you view, change, print or export is recorded.
