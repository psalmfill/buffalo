**You see:** your whole state, up to confidential (and your own records).

## Every day

- **Verify or return** submitted incidents within the deadline:
  critical 1 h · high 6 h · medium 1 day · low 2 days.
- **Escalate** incidents needing national attention (reason required).
- Start investigations, resolve (with a summary), amend with a reason.
- **Coverage reports** waiting for you: Mark reviewed, or Return for changes.
- Read and **acknowledge** shared threats and flashpoints.
- **Delegated?** Decide access requests: Access requests → Awaiting my decision.

## Sharing

- **Disseminate** threats and flashpoints to neighbouring states or LGAs
  (reason of 20+ characters). Classification is never overridden.

## You cannot

- See secret records · close, reopen or invalidate incidents · delete
  records · revoke disseminations · decide requests without a delegation.

## Rules

- Lowest classification that protects the information.
- Large exports and sensitive downloads ask for your authenticator code.
- Everything you view, change, print or export is recorded.
