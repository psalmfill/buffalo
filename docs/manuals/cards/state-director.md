**You see:** your whole state, up to secret.

## Every day

- **Incidents:** verify or return within the deadline (critical 1 h · high
  6 h · medium 1 day · low 2 days); escalate; close resolved incidents;
  invalidate false or duplicate ones (reason required).
- **Access requests** from other states: Approve (authenticator code; may
  shorten or narrow, never widen) or Reject with a note.
- **Threats & flashpoints:** disseminate to neighbours whose area is
  affected; act on "not shared" alerts.
- **Coverage reports:** Mark reviewed (locks it) or Return for changes.
- **Suggested blackspots:** Accept or Dismiss.

## Delegation

- Access requests → Delegations → **Delegate** to your Deputy (start date,
  optional end date). Revoke any time.

## Digests

- Routine threats 07:00 · searches of your records 06:30 · blackspot
  suggestions overnight.

## Rules

- You decide who outside the state sees your persons records: grants are
  read-only, time-limited, never above the holder's clearance.
- Deleting records needs a reason and is recorded.
- Everything you view, change, print or export is recorded.
