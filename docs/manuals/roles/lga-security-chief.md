As LGA Security Chief you are the first link in BUFFALO: most information
starts with you. You see and record everything in **your own LGA**, at the
**restricted** level (and anything you created yourself).

## Your daily routine

1. Check the **dashboard** and the notification bell: returned incidents,
   threats, reminders.
2. **Report incidents** as they happen. For anything critical, use the fast
   critical report and complete it afterwards
   ([Reporting and processing an incident](../guides/reporting-incidents.md#fast-critical-report)).
3. Correct and resubmit any incident **returned** to you.
4. Add **updates** to open incidents as you learn more.
5. Record **threat reports** for warnings you receive
   ([Threat reports](../guides/threats-and-dissemination.md)).

## Weekly and periodic work

- **Coverage report** for your LGA for each period: deployments, gaps and the
  overall rating ([Coverage reports](../guides/coverage-reports.md)). You are
  reminded when one is overdue.
- **Flashpoint and blackspot reviews** when their review date comes
  ([Flashpoints](../guides/flashpoints.md)).
- **Person of interest reviews** when due.
- Keep the **LGA profile** and **communities** up to date (States & LGAs
  → LGAs → your LGA, or **New community** on States & LGAs → Communities).
  The LGA page also lists your LGA's **wards**.
- Read your **state's profile** for background (States & LGAs → States): its
  overview, profile, senatorial districts and public office holders, with
  your own LGA in the LGAs tab. You cannot edit it, and the state's
  security, organization and incident tabs are for state and national
  officers.

## What you can do

| Area | You can |
|---|---|
| Incidents | Report, edit until verified, submit, add updates and evidence |
| Persons and persons of interest | Record, update, designate persons of interest |
| Cross-state records | Request access to a record found in another state |
| Threat reports | Record and manage your own reports; request dissemination from your State Director |
| Flashpoints | Record, assess, link incidents and notes |
| Coverage reports | Write and submit for your LGA |
| Communities, institutions, notes | Record and update |
| Reports | SITREPs, weekly summaries, incident, LGA profile and state profile PDFs |

## What you cannot do

- See other LGAs' records (they are "not found" for you).
- See confidential or secret records created by others.
- Verify, return, close or invalidate incidents, or delete records: your
  State Director or Deputy does that.
- Disseminate threats yourself (use **Request dissemination**) or export
  incident lists.

## When your state is not live yet

You can complete your account setup and two-factor enrolment during training,
but you cannot sign in until your state goes live. You will then see "BUFFALO
is not yet active for your state."
