As Deputy State Director you see **everything in your state** up to
**confidential** (and anything you created). You share most of the State
Director's daily review work.

## Your daily routine

1. **Review submitted incidents** within their deadlines: verify, or return
   with a reason ([Reporting and processing an incident](../guides/reporting-incidents.md#review-verify-or-return-state-director-deputy)).
2. Escalate incidents that need national attention.
3. Review **coverage reports** waiting for you (the count is shown next to
   Coverage reports): mark reviewed, or return for changes.
4. Read new **threat reports** and **shared threats & flashpoints** from
   neighbouring states, and acknowledge them.
5. If the State Director has **delegated** access decisions to you, decide
   requests in **Access requests → Awaiting my decision**
   ([Persons and cross-state requests](../guides/persons-and-cross-state-requests.md#deciding-a-request-state-director-delegated-deputy)).

## What you can do

Everything the LGA Security Chief can, for the whole state, plus:

- verify, return, escalate incidents, start investigations, resolve, and
  amend after verification (with a reason);
- review and return coverage reports;
- disseminate threats and flashpoints to neighbouring states and LGAs;
- export incident lists and generate monthly summaries;
- decide access requests **while you hold an active delegation**.

## What you cannot do

- See secret records (unless you created them).
- Close, reopen or invalidate incidents, or reactivate expired threats.
- Delete records (Deputy State Directors hold no delete rights).
- Revoke disseminations.
- Decide access requests without a current delegation.
