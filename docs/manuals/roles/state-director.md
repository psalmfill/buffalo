As State Director you see **everything in your state**, up to **secret**.
You are accountable for the state's information: its quality, its review,
and who outside the state may see it.

## Your daily routine

1. **Dashboard:** incidents by LGA, items waiting for review, coverage status
   per LGA, sites without government security.
2. **Incidents:** verify or return submitted incidents within their deadlines;
   escalate to national level when needed; close resolved incidents
   ([Reporting and processing an incident](../guides/reporting-incidents.md)).
3. **Access requests:** decide requests from other states for your persons
   records ([Persons and cross-state requests](../guides/persons-and-cross-state-requests.md#deciding-a-request-state-director-delegated-deputy)).
   You are asked to confirm approvals with your authenticator code.
4. **Threats and flashpoints:** read new warnings; share them with neighbouring
   states or LGAs when their area reaches them
   ([Threat reports and dissemination](../guides/threats-and-dissemination.md)).
   Act on BUFFALO's daily "not shared" alerts.
5. **Coverage reports:** review, return or mark reviewed.
6. **Suggested blackspots:** accept or dismiss the night's suggestions.

## Delegating approvals

You can let your Deputy decide access requests while you are away:
**Access requests → Delegations → Delegate**, from a start date and
optionally until an end date. Revoke it at any time.

## What you can do

Everything in the state, including:

- the full incident workflow except acknowledging escalations (national);
- invalidating incidents, reopening and closing them;
- deleting records (with a reason), verifying and removing evidence;
- reactivating expired threat reports and revoking disseminations;
- exporting incident lists and generating every report type.

## What you cannot do

- See other states' records, except persons through an approved grant and
  threats or flashpoints shared with you.
- Decide a request you made yourself.
- Manage user accounts (the system administrator does that; you can view the
  users in your state).

## Daily digests you receive

- Routine threat reports (07:00).
- How often other states found your records (06:30).
- Suggested blackspots (overnight).
