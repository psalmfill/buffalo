As Deputy Director General you see everything **nationally**, or in the
**geopolitical zones assigned to you**, up to **secret**.

## Your daily routine

1. **Dashboard:** the national or zonal picture by state, escalated incidents,
   coverage compliance, critical sites.
2. **Escalated incidents** from your states: **Acknowledge** them, and follow
   their investigation ([Reporting and processing an incident](../guides/reporting-incidents.md#escalation-to-national-level)).
3. **Critical notifications:** critical incidents, immediate threats, critical
   coverage ratings, overdue verifications in your states.
4. **Access oversight:** **Access requests → Oversight** lists requests and
   grants across your states; escalated requests can be decided by you
   ([Persons and cross-state requests](../guides/persons-and-cross-state-requests.md)).
5. **Exports review** (Administration): who exported what, when, and at which
   classification. Unusual export volumes are reported to you.

## What you can do

- Everything the State Director can, across your states, except deciding
  requests that have not been escalated.
- Acknowledge escalations; reopen, close and invalidate incidents.
- Analytics, including cross-jurisdiction activity.

## What you cannot do

- See states outside your zones (if zones are assigned).
- Receive "record found" notices: national users already see every record in
  their reach.
- Manage user accounts.
