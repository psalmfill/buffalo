## Generating a report

Open **Overview → Reports** and choose the report type:

| Report | Format | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Daily situation report (SITREP) | PDF | All intelligence roles |
| Weekly summary | PDF | All intelligence roles |
| Monthly summary | PDF | DG, DDG, State Director, Deputy |
| Incident list export | Spreadsheet (XLSX) or CSV | DG, DDG, State Director, Deputy |
| Incident report, LGA profile, coverage report | PDF | Anyone who can open the record |
| Analytics report | PDF | Anyone who can open those analytics |

Choose the period and place, then **Generate**. Reports are built in the
background; you are notified when yours is ready (and the page updates by
itself). Download it from **Reports**.

Reports are built **with your own access**: they only contain what you may
see, at the time they are generated.

## What every file carries

- The classification of the most sensitive content included, at the top and
  bottom of every page.
- "Generated for [your name, role, jurisdiction] on [date and time]", a
  reference (`RPT-…`) and page numbers; PDFs also carry your name as a
  watermark.
- Spreadsheets carry the same marking in their first rows.

Treat a printed or downloaded report like the original records: keep it
secure and destroy it when no longer needed.

## Limits

- **No names of persons** in bulk files. The incident list gives counts of
  people involved and no free-text descriptions. LGA profiles name only the
  current LGA office holders.
- **No evidence** in any export.
- Incident lists: at most 50,000 rows; above 10,000 you are asked to confirm.
- Daily quotas apply per user. Large exports and secret reports ask you to
  confirm with your authenticator code (step-up). Unusual export volumes are
  reported to the national level.

## Downloads and expiry

- Only **you** can download a report you generated. Every download is
  recorded.
- Files are kept for **30 days**, then deleted; generate it again if needed.
- Emails never contain report files, only a message that the report is ready.

## Scheduled reports

On **Reports → Scheduled reports**, schedule a SITREP, weekly or monthly summary to
be generated for you automatically. Schedules pause if you are moved to
another post or your account is deactivated.

## Printing a record

**Print** on an incident produces a print view with the classification at
the top and bottom of every page and "Printed by [you] on [date]". Each print
is recorded.

## Analytics

**Overview → Analytics** shows charts for incidents, casualties, response,
threats and flashpoints, coverage and (nationally) cross-jurisdiction
activity, with filters for period, place and incident type. Chart exports are
marked and recorded like reports.
