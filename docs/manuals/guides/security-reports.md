A security report is an LGA's written account of its security situation,
with photos and videos if you have them. Everything else (a title, the
period, a rating, the agencies deployed) is optional.

## Writing a report (LGA Security Chief, State Director, Deputy)

Open **Security → Security reports → New report**:

1. Choose the LGA (LGA chiefs: their own).
2. Write the **Report**. It can be long: up to 50,000 characters, with
   headings, lists and simple tables. The counter below the box shows how
   much is left.
3. **Save draft**. You can then add **photos and videos**: JPEG, PNG or WebP
   photos up to 25 MB and MP4 or WebM videos up to 100 MB, at most 30 per
   report, each with an optional caption. Files are checked for malware
   before they are accepted (large files are checked right after upload and
   shown once the check passes) and kept exactly as uploaded. Photos are
   shown without their location and camera details.
4. If you want, open **More details (optional)**: a title, the report date
   (today unless changed), the period (ad hoc unless you choose weekly,
   monthly or quarterly), the overall rating, gaps, recommendations, the
   agencies deployed and the communities they cover, and the classification.
5. **Submit for review**. Only the report text is needed. The State Director
   and Deputy are notified; a **critical** rating also notifies the DG and
   the DDGs covering the state.

There can be any number of ad hoc reports. Weekly, monthly and quarterly
reports remain one per LGA and period.

When you enter deployments, the report also shows totals by agency, the
communities left uncovered and the change since the previous report.

## Review (State Director, Deputy)

Reports waiting for you are counted next to **Security reports** in the menu.

- **Return for changes** with a reason: the report, and its photos and
  videos, can be edited again and the submitter is notified.
- **Mark reviewed** with an optional comment. A reviewed report, with its
  attachments, is **locked** and cannot be changed by anyone.

## Who can see a report

Security reports follow BUFFALO's jurisdiction rules strictly: an LGA chief
sees only their own LGA's reports, State Directors and Deputies only their
own state's, DDGs their zones (or all states), the DG all. They are never
shared across states. See [Who can see what](who-can-see-what.md).

## Reminders

An LGA has reported for a period when any security report was submitted
during it. If none was, after the grace days the LGA Security Chief and the
State Director (with the list of missing LGAs) are reminded, once per period.

## Discarding a draft

The creator can **Discard draft** while it is a draft.
