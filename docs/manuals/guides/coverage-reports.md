A coverage report records the security presence in one LGA for one period:
which agencies are deployed where, in what numbers, and what is not covered.

## Writing a report (LGA Security Chief, State Director, Deputy)

Open **Security → Coverage reports → New report**:

1. Choose the LGA (LGA chiefs: their own) and the period: weekly, monthly,
   quarterly or ad hoc. There is one report per LGA and period.
2. **Add deployment** for each agency presence: the agency, the formation
   (optional), personnel and vehicle counts, patrol frequency, and the
   communities covered.
3. The overall **rating**: adequate, partial, inadequate or critical, with a
   summary, and the gaps unless the rating is adequate.

The report computes totals by agency, the communities left uncovered, and
the change since the previous report.

Save it as a draft until it is ready, then **Submit**. A submitted report
needs a rating, a summary, the gaps (unless adequate) and at least one
deployment (unless critical). The State Director and Deputy are notified; a
**critical** rating also notifies the DG and the DDGs covering the state.

## Review (State Director, Deputy)

Reports waiting for you are counted next to **Coverage reports** in the menu.

- **Return for changes** with a reason: the report can be edited again and
  the submitter is notified.
- **Mark reviewed** with an optional comment. A reviewed report is **locked**
  and cannot be changed by anyone.

## Reminders

If a report for the last completed period is missing after the grace days,
the LGA Security Chief and the State Director (with the list of missing LGAs)
are reminded, once per period.

## Discarding a draft

The creator can **Discard draft** while it is a draft.
