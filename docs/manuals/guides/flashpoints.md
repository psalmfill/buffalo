A **flashpoint** is a place of recurring tension (for example a disputed
boundary or a market with frequent clashes). A **blackspot** is a place of
recurring crime (for example a stretch of road with repeated robberies).
Both are recorded as a point with a radius (100 metres to 50 kilometres).

## Recording one

Open **Threats & flashpoints → Flashpoints & blackspots → Record flashpoint**:

- kind (flashpoint or blackspot), name, LGA and the point and radius on the
  map (the point must be inside the LGA);
- threat type and description;
- classification.

## Assessments

The concern level, status and next review date change only through an
assessment (**Record assessment**), so their history is complete:

- **Concern:** low, moderate, high or severe.
- **Status:** active, monitoring, dormant or resolved.
- Recommended action and the next review date.

You are reminded when a review is due. Assessments cannot be edited
afterwards.

## Links and alerts

- Link the incidents that happened at the flashpoint (including closed ones)
  and the community security notes about it.
- When a new incident is submitted inside a flashpoint's area, the LGA chief,
  State Director and Deputy are told.
- When a blackspot keeps recording incidents (above a threshold in 30
  days), the State Director and Deputy are asked to reassess it.

## Suggested blackspots

Every night BUFFALO looks for clusters of incidents that no flashpoint or
blackspot covers yet. State Directors, Deputies, the DG and DDGs see them in
**Suggested blackspots**, with the incidents behind each. **Accept** one to
create a blackspot from it, or **Dismiss** it.

## On the map

**Overview → Operational map** shows flashpoints, blackspots and threat
reports with incident density and other layers. Flashpoints can be shared
with neighbouring jurisdictions like threat reports
([Threat reports and dissemination](threats-and-dissemination.md)).
