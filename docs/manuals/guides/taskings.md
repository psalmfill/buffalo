A tasking is a written instruction from a State Director or Deputy State
Director to the LGA Security Chiefs of their state: one LGA, several, or
every LGA. It needs an answer: each LGA acknowledges it and reports back,
and the directors see who has read and acted on it.

## Sending a tasking (State Director, Deputy State Director)

Open **New tasking** on your dashboard's **Tasking** card, **Taskings → New
tasking**, or **Send tasking** on an LGA's page (that LGA is ticked for you).

1. **Choose the LGAs.** The list shows every active LGA of your state with
   its current Security Chief, or "No chief assigned".
   - Tick one LGA, or several.
   - **Select by senatorial district** ticks every LGA of a district; use it
     again to add another district.
   - **Select all** ticks every LGA of the state. It always means the whole
     state, even when the search box is hiding some rows; the label says how
     many ("Select all 31 LGAs").
   - **Clear** unticks everything. The count ("3 of 31 LGAs selected") and
     the chips show what is chosen.
2. Write the **instruction**. It can be long (up to 50,000 characters, with
   headings, lists and simple tables); the counter shows how much is left.
   A **title** is optional; without one the tasking shows as "Tasking from
   the State Director, [date]".
3. Choose the **priority** (routine, priority or immediate) and, if you
   want, a **response due** date and time.
4. Leave the **classification** at Restricted. LGA Security Chiefs are
   cleared for Restricted only, so a tasking at a higher level could not be
   read by them: the form warns you and refuses to send it.
5. **Save draft** to keep it unsent. Once saved you can attach photos,
   videos and documents (PDF, Word, Excel, text). Files are checked for
   malware and kept exactly as uploaded.
6. **Send.** A confirmation states who receives it ("Send to the Uyo LGA
   chief", "Send to 3 LGA chiefs: Uyo, Ikot Ekpene, Eket", or "Send to all
   31 LGA chiefs in Akwa Ibom") and lists LGAs without a chief. You may send
   to those: the tasking waits for the next chief.

Each tasking gets a reference when sent, such as `TSK/AK/2026/00042`.

### After sending

A sent tasking **cannot be changed**: not its text, priority, due date,
classification or recipients. This keeps a reliable record of what was
instructed and when. Instead:

- **Add follow-up note**: an extra instruction or clarification. Notes
  cannot be edited or removed, and every recipient LGA is told.
- **Recall**, with a reason. Recipients still see the tasking, marked
  "Recalled by [name]: [reason]", and can no longer act on it. To correct
  it, send a new tasking.

### Following progress

The dashboard card and the **Taskings** list show a progress line for each
tasking, for example "12 of 31 read · 5 acknowledged · 2 completed · 3
overdue". The tasking's page has a table of every LGA (chief, status, when
read, acknowledged and completed, overdue) with filters; click a row to read
that LGA's report back and see its attachments. **Print / Download** gives
the tasking and the recipients table as a PDF.

A tasking is **overdue** for an LGA that has not completed it by the
response date. The LGA's chief is told once at that time; you get one
summary a day while any LGA is overdue. The **Taskings** menu item shows how
many of your taskings have an LGA overdue.

## Receiving a tasking (LGA Security Chief)

New taskings appear at the top of your dashboard under **Messages from the
Director** (unread ones in bold, overdue ones flagged) and in **Taskings**,
whose menu item shows how many are unread. An immediate tasking also pops
up as an alert.

Open it to read it; this tells the directors you have read it. Then:

1. **Acknowledge** (one click) to say you have it in hand.
2. When it is done, write your **report back** and **Mark completed**. The
   report back is required, and you can attach photos, videos and documents.
   Attachments can be removed until you mark it completed.
3. After completing, you can **add further responses** (they cannot be
   changed later).

The directors are told each time you acknowledge, complete or respond.

## Who sees a tasking

| Who | Sees |
|---|---|
| LGA Security Chief | Taskings addressed to **your own LGA** only, and only your LGA's status and report back. You never see which other LGAs received it or what they answered. |
| State Director, Deputy | Every tasking of your state, every LGA's status and report back. |
| DG, DDG | Every tasking within your reach, read-only. |
| Super admin | None. |

A tasking belongs to the **LGA**, not the person: if a chief is reposted,
their successor sees the LGA's taskings. Taskings never cross states: they
cannot be shared or granted to anyone outside the state.
